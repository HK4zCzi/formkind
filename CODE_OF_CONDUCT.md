# Code of Conduct

FormKind is built for human variety, and its community should be welcoming too.

## Our standards

Be respectful, specific, and curious. Welcome people of every background and experience level. Critique ideas and code, not people. Respect privacy, preferred names and pronouns, and cultural differences. Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project owner through the contact link on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Do not open a public issue containing sensitive details. The maintainer will acknowledge reports promptly, protect confidentiality where possible, and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant.

This code applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the project. Enforcement decisions may be appealed once with new context.
